# Document 07 — Camera System & Cinematic Direction

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Camera System defines how visitors experience SYNAPSE.

Rather than acting as a viewport, the camera behaves like an invisible cinematographer.

Every movement communicates emotion, guides attention, and reinforces storytelling.

Visitors should never consciously think about the camera.

They should simply feel immersed.

---

# Philosophy

The camera is not controlled.

It is guided.

The visitor influences the journey.

The camera tells the story.

The relationship is collaborative rather than mechanical.

# Camera Modes

## Idle

Used before interaction.

Almost imperceptible floating.

Subtle breathing motion.

---

## Discovery

Camera slowly searches.

Curves reveal hidden objects.

Never rushes.

---

## Focus

Moves toward important elements.

Stops comfortably.

Maintains readable framing.

---

## Orbit

Used during project showcases.

Slow.

Elegant.

Natural.

Never constant speed.

---

## Transition

Bridges two narrative moments.

No cuts.

No teleports.

Everything feels physically connected.

---

## Conversation

Digital Jaasim becomes the focal point.

Camera behaves as though another person is standing beside the visitor.

Respectful distance.

Comfortable framing.

---

## Ending

Camera gradually expands perspective.

The universe feels infinite.

# Focus System

Depth of field supports storytelling.

Only the narrative subject receives sharp focus.

Everything else softly fades.

Focus shifts naturally.

Never instantly.

The camera never travels in straight lines.

Movement follows smooth splines.

Organic curves.

Natural arcs.

Gentle overshoot.

Cause motion sickness.

Rotate aggressively.

---

# Common Failure Modes

Avoid:

* Direct camera manipulation inside scenes.
* Multiple systems writing to camera transforms.
* Sudden changes in FOV.
* Constant-speed movements.
* Circular orbit loops.
* Robotic easing.
* Excessive camera shake.
* Aggressive rotations.
* Camera clipping.
* Losing the subject during transitions.

---

# Engineering Review Checklist

Before approving the Camera System:

✔ One owner controls the camera.

✔ All movements use spline-based paths.

✔ Every movement has easing and settling.

✔ Reverse timeline behaves correctly.

✔ AI navigation feels cinematic.

✔ Composition follows framing rules.

✔ Camera never clips.

✔ Accessibility mode functions correctly.

✔ Debug tools expose all camera state.

✔ Every frame feels intentional.
