# PROJECT SYNAPSE

# Document 03 — Visual Design System & Art Direction

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document establishes the visual identity of SYNAPSE.

It defines every artistic principle, design language, material system, lighting rule, motion philosophy, shader direction, and rendering style.

Every future asset, animation, and shader must comply with this document.

---

# Design Philosophy

SYNAPSE should not feel designed.

It should feel discovered.

Visitors should feel as if they have entered a hidden universe that has existed long before they arrived.

Nothing should appear manufactured.

Everything should appear to have naturally evolved.

# UI Philosophy

UI should disappear.

The world is the interface.

Buttons should feel embedded into the environment.

Menus should almost not exist.

---

# Cursor

Cursor is not an arrow.

Cursor becomes light.

It softly illuminates nearby particles.

Nearby fog reacts.

Objects subtly acknowledge its presence.

Soft.

# Fog

Fog is a character.

Not an effect.

Fog guides attention.

Separates depth.

Reacts to movement.

Flows slowly.

Never blocks important content.

---

# Particle Philosophy

Particles are alive.

Never random.

Every particle has purpose.

---

Particle Types

Knowledge

Curiosity

Memory

Energy

Navigation

Celebration

---

Particle Behavior

Avoid cursor slightly.

Gather around important objects.

Follow invisible flow fields.

Never jitter.

Never teleport.

Never like a game camera.

---

Camera Rules

Everything has inertia.

Nothing starts instantly.

Nothing stops instantly.

Everything has weight.

Even light has momentum.

# Reflection Rules

Only important objects reflect.

Reflection indicates significance.

Reflection is never decorative.

---

# Shader Philosophy

Shaders should enhance emotion.

Never exist merely to impress.

---

Allowed Effects

Fresnel

Subtle refraction

Vertex displacement

Procedural noise

Flow maps

Soft dissolve

Glow

Light scattering

Volumetric lighting

Screen-space reflections

Heat distortion (minimal)

Energy pulses

---

Avoid

Rainbow gradients

Heavy chromatic aberration

Extreme bloom

Lens dirt

Aggressive glitches

Excessive distortion

Cheap hologram effects

---

# Post Processing

Minimal.

Invisible.

Professional.

---

Allowed

Bloom

Ambient Occlusion

Depth of Field

Tone Mapping

Subtle Vignette

Temporal Anti-Aliasing

Volumetric Lighting

---

Never over-process.

Visitors should notice the world,

not the effects.

---

# Performance Rules

Every visual choice must justify its GPU cost.

If two techniques produce the same emotional impact,

choose the cheaper one.

Beauty through efficiency.

---

# Accessibility

Visual effects must never reduce readability.

Motion reduction mode available.

High contrast mode available.

Keyboard navigation supported.

---

# The SYNAPSE Rule

Every visual decision must answer:

"Does this make the universe feel more alive?"

If yes,

keep it.

If not,

remove it.

---

# Final Artistic Statement

SYNAPSE should look less like software,

and more like discovering a living ecosystem built from thoughts, memories, curiosity, and engineering.

The visitor should never feel they are navigating a website.

They should feel they have briefly stepped into a world that exists somewhere just beyond reality.
