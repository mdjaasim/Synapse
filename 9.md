# PROJECT SYNAPSE

# Document 08 — Scene Director & World Orchestration System

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Scene Director is the highest-level runtime coordinator responsible for orchestrating the living world of SYNAPSE.

While the Camera System determines *how* the visitor sees the world, and the Scroll Engine determines *when* the experience progresses, the Scene Director determines *what happens* inside the world.

It behaves like a film director, constantly coordinating actors, lighting, environments, camera intentions, particles, shaders, AI interactions, and narrative pacing.

Without the Scene Director, the experience becomes a collection of independent animations.

With it, the world behaves like one cohesive organism.

---

# Philosophy

Nothing happens independently.

Everything happens because the Scene Director decided it should.

The world behaves intentionally.

Never randomly.

---

# Responsibilities

The Scene Director owns:

* Scene lifecycle
* Narrative pacing
* World orchestration
* Environmental reactions
* Project activation
* AI world integration
* Visual emphasis
* Narrative transitions
* Scene synchronization

The Scene Director does **NOT** own:

Camera movement

Rendering

Physics

Shader execution

Particle simulation

Instead, it coordinates these systems through commands and events.
