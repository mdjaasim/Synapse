# Purpose

This document defines the complete visitor journey through SYNAPSE.

It specifies:

* Story progression
* Camera choreography
* Scroll progression
* User interactions
* Emotional pacing
* Scene objectives

Future implementation documents must reference this document as the canonical timeline.

---

# Experience Duration

Average Experience

5–8 minutes

Explorer Mode

15–25 minutes

Completionists

30+ minutes (discovering easter eggs, AI conversations, hidden interactions)

The goal is **not** to rush visitors.

The goal is to encourage exploration.

---

# Scroll Philosophy

Scrolling does **not** move the webpage.

Scrolling advances the cinematic timeline.

Imagine dragging the playhead of a film.

Forward scroll → Time advances.

Backward scroll → Time rewinds.

The visitor always feels in control.

# Mouse Philosophy

Cursor influences light.

Particles avoid cursor.

Fog reacts slightly.

Hover never feels binary.

Everything should interpolate smoothly.

---

# Cinematic Rules

No scene begins instantly.

Every transition is motivated.

No camera teleportation.

Every movement has weight.

Every object feels physical.

Every animation respects inertia.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The experience succeeds when:

Visitors lose awareness that they are on a website.

They feel like they explored a living world.

They understand Mohamed Jaasim without reading long paragraphs.

They remember at least one unforgettable moment.

They want to revisit.

They share the experience with someone else.
