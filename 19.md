Purpose

The World Physics System defines how the SYNAPSE universe behaves independently of the visitor.

Unlike traditional websites where nothing happens unless the user interacts, SYNAPSE is a living ecosystem.

Objects influence each other.

Materials react.

Energy flows.

Knowledge grows.

Memory evolves.

The visitor is not controlling the world.

The visitor is entering an already living universe.

Philosophy

Nothing waits for the visitor.

The universe existed before they arrived.

The universe will continue existing after they leave.

Visitors merely influence it.

Final Statement

The World Physics System transforms SYNAPSE from a rendered environment into a living digital ecosystem.
